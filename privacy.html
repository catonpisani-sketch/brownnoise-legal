Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Brown Noise for Sleep + Relax ("the App") handles, where it is kept, and the rights you have. In short: the App has no server of its own and does not use any analytics, advertising or tracking service. What you enter in the App stays on your device.

1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

The data controller is Catón Adrian Pisani Espejo, an individual developer based in Spain.
Address: Calle del Marqués de Viana, 82, 28039 Madrid, Spain
NIF: Y7749863G

Contact: brownnoiseforsleep@proton.me

2. Information That Stays on Your Device

  • Setup answers. The App asks a few questions during setup (your general goal, age range, gender, typical sleep hours, how often you wake at night, and your interests) to personalize your default settings. The answers are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Favorites and settings. Your favorite sounds, sleep-timer length, sound intensity and similar preferences are stored only on your device.
  • Your name for sign-in. If you sign in, the App keeps the name it shows you (or your email address, if no name is shared) and which sign-in method you used, only on your device. See section 3.

We have no access to any of this information.

3. Signing In

You can sign in with Apple or with Google. The App connects directly to the provider you choose and receives only the name and email address you allow it to share; Apple lets you hide your email behind a private relay address. This is used only to show your name in the App. Nothing is sent to us. Your use of these services is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions, free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your subscription status with Apple on your device to unlock Premium. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of this information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple provides us with aggregated sales and usage reports (such as numbers of downloads, trials and subscriptions) that do not identify you.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules a local reminder on your device before your free trial ends. It is created and delivered on your device; no server or push service is involved, and we never see whether you receive or open it. Delivery depends on your device's notification settings and is not guaranteed, so please keep track of your trial end date yourself as well.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services in the App.
  • We do not sell or share your information, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The App does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location, and does not ask for those permissions.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Because your information stays on your device, you are in control of it:

  • Settings → Delete Account deletes your sign-in link and your favorites from the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device.
  • You can stop using your Apple ID with the App in your device's Settings, and remove the App's access in your Google Account settings.

Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. For any question or request, email brownnoiseforsleep@proton.me. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

8. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you have a question about a child's use of the App, contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when the App changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, inform you in the App or on this page before they take effect. If a future version of the App needs your consent for something, it will ask you first; continued use of the App will not be treated as consent.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to brownnoiseforsleep@proton.me.
