Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Brown Noise for Sleep + Relax ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is designed to work entirely on your device — it does not have a server, and information you enter is not transmitted to us unless stated otherwise below.

1. Information You Provide

  • Sign-in information. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only the name and email address you authorize the provider to share (Apple lets you hide your email using a private relay address). This is used solely to identify your session within the App and is stored only on your device.
  • Onboarding answers. The App asks a few questions during setup (e.g. your general goal, age range, gender, typical sleep hours, and how often you wake at night) to personalize your default settings. These answers are stored only in the App's local storage on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Favorites and settings. Which sounds you favorite, your preferred sleep-timer length, intensity, and similar preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not operate any server or database, so none of the information above is transmitted to, or stored by, us.
  • The current version of the App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking service. If a future version adds analytics, this policy will be updated first, and any data collected will be limited to anonymous, non-personally-identifying usage statistics.
  • We do not access your microphone, camera, or location, and the App does not request those permissions.

3. Notifications

If you allow it, the App schedules a local reminder on your device before your free trial ends. This is generated and delivered entirely on-device; no server or push service is involved, and we never see whether or how you respond to it.

4. Payments

Subscriptions are billed and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of payment information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Sign in with Apple: governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sign in with Google: governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not share any information with these providers beyond the standard authentication handshake required to sign you in.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are always in control of it. You can delete your locally stored favorites and account link at any time from Settings → Delete Account, and uninstalling the App removes all of its local data from your device.

If you have questions about this policy or how the App handles information, contact us at brownnoiseforsleep@proton.me.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will address it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to brownnoiseforsleep@proton.me.
