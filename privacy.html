Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Brown Noise for Sleep + Relax ("the App") collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the rights you have. The App has no server of its own: most information stays on your device. The exception is anonymous usage analytics, described in section 3.

2. Information That Stays on Your Device

  • Onboarding answers. The App asks a few questions during setup (your general goal, age range, gender, typical sleep hours, how often you wake at night, and your interests) to personalize your default settings. These answers are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, including our analytics provider.
  • Sign-in information. If you sign in with Apple or Google, the App receives only the name and email address you allow the provider to share (Apple lets you hide your email behind a private relay address). It is used to show your name in the App and is stored only on your device.
  • Favorites and settings. Your favorite sounds, sleep-timer length, intensity and similar preferences are stored only on your device.

3. Anonymous Usage Analytics

To understand how the App is used and improve it, the App sends usage events to our analytics provider, MostlyGoodMetrics. Each event contains:

  • What happened in the App, for example that a setup screen was shown or completed, that the subscription screen was opened or closed, that a purchase was started, completed, cancelled or failed (with a general reason category, never payment details), that sign-in succeeded or failed (and with which provider), and which sound and intensity were played, for roughly how long, and the sleep-timer length.
  • A random installation ID, created by the App the first time it opens. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple or Google account, or advertising identifier.
  • A random session ID, new each time the App opens.
  • Technical details: platform (iOS), iOS version, App version and build, language and region setting, and whether the build is a development or production build.
  • Approximate country. MostlyGoodMetrics derives the country from the internet (IP) address of each request. We have configured it to keep the country only; it does not keep your region, city or IP address.

Analytics events never include your onboarding answers, your name or email, payment details, or any free text you type.

Purpose and legal basis. We use these events to measure how people move through setup and the subscription screen, find problems, and decide what to improve. We only send them with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and Art. 22.2 of Spain's Law 34/2002 (LSSI), because the App stores the installation ID on your device). The App asks the first time you open it, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data. If you say no, no analytics events are sent and the App works exactly the same.

Retention. Analytics events are kept while our MostlyGoodMetrics account is active, so we can compare usage over time. If we close that account, MostlyGoodMetrics deletes the data within 14 days. We delete the events of a specific installation earlier on request (see section 7).

Where the data is processed. MostlyGoodMetrics stores the data with its hosting providers Neon (database) and Fly.io (application hosting), and uses MaxMind to look up the country from the IP address. This may involve processing outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. 4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules a local reminder on your device before your free trial ends. It is created and delivered on your device; no server or push service is involved, and we never see whether you receive or open it. Delivery depends on your device's notification settings and is not guaranteed, so please keep track of your trial end date yourself as well.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are billed and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of payment information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Who Receives Information

  • MostlyGoodMetrics, our analytics provider, receives the analytics events described in section 3 and processes them on our behalf, only to provide our analytics. [MostlyGoodMetrics publishes no data processing agreement and doesn't state its role; get a signed DPA (GDPR Art. 28) before publishing — ask support@mostlygoodmetrics.com]
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple and App Store payments: Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google, if you choose Sign in with Google: Google's Privacy Policy. The App exchanges only what is needed to sign you in.

We do not sell your information, do not use it for advertising, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy of it. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email brownnoiseforsleep@proton.me.

  • On your device: Settings → Delete Account deletes your sign-in link, favorites and settings. Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device, including the installation ID; a reinstall starts with a new, unrelated ID.
  • Analytics data: analytics events are linked only to a random installation ID, so we cannot find your events from your name or email (GDPR Art. 11). To access or delete them, copy your installation ID from Settings → Privacy → Installation ID and email it to us. We will ask MostlyGoodMetrics to delete or export the events with that ID and confirm within one month. Turning off Share anonymous usage data stops new events immediately.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. Children below the age of digital consent in their country (14 in Spain under Organic Law 3/2018, Art. 7; between 13 and 16 elsewhere in the EU under GDPR Art. 8) cannot consent to analytics themselves: a parent or guardian must decide on their behalf, and otherwise Share anonymous usage data should stay off. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when the App changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, inform you in the App or on this page before they take effect. Where a change requires your consent, we will ask for it and will not treat continued use of the App as consent.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to brownnoiseforsleep@proton.me.
